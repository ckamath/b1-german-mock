## 1. Scale: revenue, profitability, valuation, customers, employees, structure, ownership, marketing spend

### Takeaway
No confirmed current group revenue, profit or valuation exists in the sources found; the last hard revenue numbers are about a decade old (EUR 497m in 2015/16, EUR 652m in 2016/17) and the only valuation figure (EUR 15bn) is one journalist's extrapolation. What is reasonably established is the shape: a founder/management-controlled Munich group of roughly 90 near-autonomous GmbHs, each with its own P&L, with a very large marketing budget (EUR 171m gross in 2018 per Nielsen, reportedly over EUR 150m on TV alone in 2022).

## 2. Verticals: relative importance and seasonality

### Takeaway
Check24 spans insurance, finance/banking, energy, telecoms, travel, shopping, handyman services and more, organised as independent vertical companies. Motor insurance is the origin and the most visibly seasonal product: the switching season ends 30 November, and about 1.7 million motor contracts changed provider in the 2025 season, with comparison portals accounting for a bit over a quarter of new business.

### Cited Findings
- Product scope since 2008: insurance, finance, energy, telecommunications, travel, shopping, handicrafts (Handwerker) and aesthetic surgery — [Wikipedia DE](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Check24)
- Motor insurance was the first product (October 1999) and Check24 was market leader in motor insurance comparison by 2010 — [Wikipedia DE](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Check24)
- Seasonality: the "hottest phase" of the motor switching season runs to 30 November each year; Check24 raises affiliate payouts for it (2025 campaign) — [Check24 Partnerprogramm](https://www.check24-partnerprogramm.de/affiliate-marketing/ankuendigungen/kfz-wechselsaison-2025-mit-70-euro-provision-pro-sale/)
- 2025 season volume: Sirius Campus (survey 1-11 Dec 2025, n=2,048 motor customers) counted about 1.7 million motor contracts changing provider for the 1 Jan 2026 renewal date; five providers took almost half of new contracts — [Das Investment](https://www.dasinvestment.com/kfz-versicherung-wechselsaison-vertragswechsler-sirius-campus/)
- 2025 season channels (same study, as summarised in the search snippet; wording was ambiguous): agencies, insurers' own direct online/phone sales, and comparison portals ("mostly Check24 or Verivox") each won a bit over a quarter of new business — [Das Investment](https://www.dasinvestment.com/kfz-versicherung-wechselsaison-vertragswechsler-sirius-campus/)
- Check24's current motor landing page (November 2026) advertises "up to 81% cheaper" — [Check24 Kfz](https://www.check24.de/kfz-versicherung/)
- Travel: Check24 is treated by the travel trade press as an OTA; its chatbot covers travel, and the GenDev challenge names Pauschalreise (package holidays), Flug (flights) and hotel search as verticals — [fvw](https://www.fvw.de/touristik/vertrieb/ich-waere-vorsichtig-check24-chatbot-wirbt-fuer-konkurrenz--und-teilt-gegen-ota-aus-263932); [Check24 GitHub](https://github.com/check24-scholarships/check24-chatgpt-app-challenge)
- Rental cars and tyres/car parts exist as separate verticals with their own chatbot product teams — [Check24 job ad](https://jobs.check24.de/en/jobs/produkt-projektmanagement/REF615Y-junior-produktmanager-mwd-ai-chatbot-mietwagen/); [Reifenpresse, 8 Oct 2024](https://reifenpresse.de/2024/10/08/neuer-chatbot-soll-check24-kunden-im-such-und-kaufprozess-unterstuetzen/)
- Banking: C24 Bank is a Check24 subsidiary with its own BaFin banking licence — [Versicherungsbote](https://www.versicherungsbote.de/id/4893026/Check24-mit-Banklizenz/). In November 2025 BaFin appointed a special commissioner (Sonderbeauftragter) at C24 Bank for "massive deficiencies in money-laundering prevention", and Finanztip withdrew its recommendation — [Finanztip](https://www.finanztip.de/erfahrungen/c24-bank/). From 1 Nov 2026 C24 raised its interest rate to 1% p.a. and got a new managing director — [Mobiflip](https://www.mobiflip.de/?p=494199)
- C24 runs on the Mambu core-banking platform — [FF News](https://ffnews.com/newsarticle/german-challenger-c24-extends-partnership-with-mambu-after-a-successful-launch-of-the-bank/)

### Inferences
- Verticals fall into two economic types: recurring-contract products (insurance, energy, telecoms, credit, banking) where switching recurs and trail commission or repeat switching is possible, and transaction products (travel, shopping, rental cars, tyres, handyman) with one-off margins. The first group is where automatic, agent-driven switching is most natural.
- Motor insurance concentrates demand into roughly eight weeks before 30 November, which is why TV pressure and affiliate payouts peak then.

- C24 Bank customer numbers, deposits and losses: not found (search returned nothing; the Bundesanzeiger filing was not reachable).
- Total number of verticals/products and details on real estate, shopping marketplace GMV, and handyman ("Profis") volumes: not found.

## 3. Economics: commissions, CPA, trail commission, bonuses, guarantees

### Takeaway
Check24 is paid by providers on successful conclusion (commission/courtage embedded in the price, nothing charged to the consumer) and refuses to disclose rates. The only hard numbers are what it pays downstream affiliates, EUR 70 per brokered motor policy in the 2025 season and "up to EUR 125 and more" per sale across products, which set a floor under what providers must be paying Check24.

### Cited Findings
- Model: Check24 receives a commission from the product provider on successful brokerage; in insurance it is contained in the premium and not paid separately by the customer; commission is earned only after a tariff is actually concluded — [Check24 "Über uns"](https://www.check24.de/unternehmen/ueber-uns/?product=2)
- Non-disclosure: Check24 does not want to disclose the level of its commissions — [Versicherungsbote](https://www.versicherungsbote.de/tag/Vergleichsportal/step/3/)
- Affiliate payout, motor: Check24 paid affiliate partners EUR 70.00 per successfully brokered motor insurance contract during the 2025 switching season (to 30 Nov 2025) — [Check24 Partnerprogramm](https://www.check24-partnerprogramm.de/affiliate-marketing/ankuendigungen/kfz-wechselsaison-2025-mit-70-euro-provision-pro-sale/)
- Affiliate payout, general: partner websites can earn "up to EUR 125.00 and more" per sale — [Check24 Partnerprogramm](https://www.check24.de/partner/partnerprogramm/)
- "Nirgendwo-Günstiger-Garantie": after a first ruling in 2018 Check24 reshaped the guarantee so that it reimburses the extra cost if a customer finds a cheaper offer — [Versicherungsbote](https://www.versicherungsbote.de/id/4881134/Check24-Nirgendwo-Guenstiger-HUK-Coburg/). In a later ruling the court found Check24 could offer the cheapest tariff in only about 80% of cases (see section 6) — [Horizont](https://www.horizont.net/marketing/nachrichten/werbeversprechen-gericht-verbietetcheck24die-nirgendwo-guenstiger-garantie-182526)
- Discount promotions in insurance are legally constrained: LG München I ruled Check24's "Jubiläumsdeals" for insurance inadmissible (commission-rebate ban, Sondervergütungsverbot) on a BVK complaint — [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/lg-muenchen-check-24-jubilaeumsdeals-unzulaessig-rabatte-versicherungen-online-bkv)
- Bundeskartellamt finding (2019): some portals ordered results by the level of commission received from listed providers — [AssCompact](https://www.asscompact.de/nachrichten/vergleichsportale-bundeskartellamt-identifiziert-sieben-problemfelder)
- Benchmark from the auto-switching niche: Wechselpilot says it sometimes receives commissions from intermediaries and often passes part on to customers as a bonus — [Wechselpilot](https://www.wechselpilot.com/hilfe/wechselservice/wie-finanziert-sich-wechselpilot/)

### Inferences
- If Check24 can pay an affiliate EUR 70 per motor policy and still earn a margin, the insurer-side commission per policy is materially above EUR 70. This is a lower bound by logic, not a sourced figure.
- Because the commission sits inside the price and is not shown, the consumer cannot see the cost of distribution. An agent acting for the consumer would have reason to surface or rebate it; in insurance the Sondervergütungsverbot limits rebating, in energy it does not (see Stiftung Warentest's criterion in section 7).

### Gaps
- Actual commission levels per vertical (per energy switch, per motor policy, per loan as % of volume, per DSL/mobile contract, travel margin) were not found in any source. Unverified recollection of commonly cited ranges: roughly EUR 40-100+ per energy contract, roughly EUR 60-130 per motor policy, around 1-3% of loan volume; treat as hypotheses to verify.
- Whether and where Check24 receives trail commission (Bestandsprovision), for example on non-motor insurance held in its brokerage portfolio: not found.
- Mechanics and size of Check24 Punkte, cashback, switching bonuses and the Check24 card: not found in this session.
- Take rate or CPA structures in shopping/marketplace and travel: not found.

## 4. Beyond comparison: account, switching services, bank, AI assistants, MCP / ChatGPT

### Takeaway
Check24 already runs an AI assistant ("Sophie") across verticals and is explicitly building toward agents that resolve customer matters on their own; in 2026 it publicly framed a ChatGPT app built on OpenAI's Apps SDK and MCP, with one MCP server per vertical behind a single proxy. No press release confirming a live, listed Check24 ChatGPT app or a public API/MCP endpoint was found.

### Cited Findings
- Customer account: the app lets users manage all contracts, bookings, policies and purchases in one personal Kundenkonto — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/id719610089)
- Sophie (third-party vendor blog, August 2026, not an official Check24 source and not read in full): answers questions around the clock, takes the customer's own cases into account after login, accepts documents, and hands over to human advisers when needed; there are domain variants such as a hotel assistant and a tyres/car-parts version — [LoyJoy blog](https://www.loyjoy.com/en/blog/check24-sophie-ai-customer-service-agent/)
- Tyres/car parts: a chatbot introduced by October 2024 also supports the search and purchase process, not only service — [Reifenpresse, 8 Oct 2024](https://reifenpresse.de/2024/10/08/neuer-chatbot-soll-check24-kunden-im-such-und-kaufprozess-unterstuetzen/)
- Agent ambition: a Check24 job ad for a product manager "AI Chatbot Mietwagen" states the goal of resolving customer matters in chat as simply, quickly and automatically as possible, by developing AI agents that increasingly resolve matters independently; the degree of automation is a named KPI (no value given) — [Check24 Karriere](https://jobs.check24.de/en/jobs/produkt-projektmanagement/REF615Y-junior-produktmanager-mwd-ai-chatbot-mietwagen/)
- Failure mode: the travel trade press reported that Sophie, when asked, recommended bricks-and-mortar travel agencies in Hamburg and made critical remarks about OTAs ("Ich wäre vorsichtig") — [fvw](https://www.fvw.de/touristik/vertrieb/ich-waere-vorsichtig-check24-chatbot-wirbt-fuer-konkurrenz--und-teilt-gegen-ota-aus-263932)
- ChatGPT app / MCP (primary source, fetched in full): the take-home challenge for the 9th round of Check24's GenDev scholarship (applications closed 17 May 2026; repository archived 31 Aug 2026) asks applicants to build a proxy MCP that gives ChatGPT a single connector endpoint in front of several independent vertical MCP servers. The README uses "hotel search through CHECK24 inside ChatGPT" via OpenAI's Apps SDK as its example — [Check24 GitHub](https://github.com/check24-scholarships/check24-chatgpt-app-challenge)
- Same source, architecture statements: ChatGPT expects one MCP connector, but each vertical owns its own MCP server; vertical teams define their own tools and schemas; the proxy must filter or adapt non-conforming tools and give teams feedback; tool-name ambiguity between Pauschalreise and Flug is the worked example — [Check24 GitHub](https://github.com/check24-scholarships/check24-chatgpt-app-challenge)
- Same source, strategy statements: customer requests often start from a life situation or goal rather than a chosen product, so one conversation may span several verticals; listed ChatGPT apps go through review and approval covers the app as submitted, so versioning and stability matter — [Check24 GitHub](https://github.com/check24-scholarships/check24-chatgpt-app-challenge)
- Context on general-purpose assistants as competitors: a 2026 test of AI assistants for interest-rate comparison found they often did not know the best savings accounts, with ChatGPT especially weak — [echo24](https://www.echo24.de/welt/ki-beim-zinsvergleich-staerken-und-schwaechen-im-test-zr-94206535.html)

### Inferences
- A recruiting challenge is not a product launch, but its specificity (app review, per-vertical MCP servers, versioning) strongly suggests Check24 was actively building or operating a ChatGPT app in the first half of 2026. Treat "Check24 has a live ChatGPT app" as probable but unconfirmed.
- Check24's org structure (independent verticals) is its main obstacle to an agent-native experience: an agent conversation starts from a life goal and crosses verticals, while tools, schemas and P&Ls are owned vertical by vertical. Check24 itself names this tension.
- Sophie today is mainly a service and pre-sales assistant inside Check24's own surfaces. The step to an agent that holds a mandate and switches contracts without the user initiating each time is not evidenced for Check24 itself.

### Gaps
- No official Check24 press release on Sophie's launch date, underlying model/vendor, volumes or automation rate was found.
- No confirmation of a publicly listed Check24 ChatGPT app, a public MCP server, or a partner/agent API.
- Vertragsmanager, energy Wechselservice / automatic re-switching, "Wechselalarm"-type reminders, Check24 Punkte and the Check24/C24 card: search returned no usable source. Unverified recollection: Check24 offers an opt-in energy switching service that re-compares before the contract year ends; verify before use.

## 5. Supply side: who refuses to be listed, coverage limits, data integration

### Takeaway
HUK-Coburg, Germany's largest motor insurer, is the standing adversary and has litigated against Check24 for years, including over how Check24 presents insurers that are not in its comparison; the Bundeskartellamt found market coverage on comparison portals "sometimes thin" and noted that portals in every sector share data bases and tariff calculators. Technical detail on how Check24 ingests tariffs was not found.

### Cited Findings
- HUK-Coburg v. Check24, OLG Köln (6 U 191/18): HUK won; summarised as "no price comparison without a price", concerning how Check24 displayed an insurer whose prices it did not show — [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/olg-koeln-6u191-18-check24-huk-coburg-versicherung-vergleich-preis)
- Counter-litigation: Check24 successfully attacked a cancellation clause of HUK24 (HUK's online arm) — [Versicherungsbote](https://www.versicherungsbote.de/id/4890174/Check24-rasiert-Huk24-wegen-Kundigungsklausel/)
- HUK-Coburg's challenge concerning Check24's rating grades for insurers failed at the ECJ "for now" — [Versicherungsbote](https://www.versicherungsbote.de/id/4939457/Check24-EuGH-weist-Klage-der-Huk-Coburg/)
- Market coverage: the Bundeskartellamt's 2019 inquiry reported sometimes thin market coverage on comparison portals — [AssCompact](https://www.asscompact.de/nachrichten/vergleichsportale-bundeskartellamt-identifiziert-sieben-problemfelder)
- Shared infrastructure: in all sectors examined there are cooperations between different comparison portals on data base and tariff calculators (white-label calculators) — [Bundeskartellamt report page](https://www.bundeskartellamt.de/SharedDocs/Publikation/DE/Sektoruntersuchungen/Sektoruntersuchung_Vergleichsportale_Bericht.html)
- Consumer-association view: the Bund der Versicherten (BdV) advises against using the popular comparison portals for switching motor insurance — [Focus Versicherungsprofi](https://versicherungsprofi.online/sparten-produkte/komposit-und-rechtsschutzversicherung/bdv-raet-bei-kfz-versicherungswechsel-von-beliebten-vergleichsportalen-ab_02377/)
- Coverage evidence from court: a Cologne court found Check24 could offer the cheapest tariff in only about 80% of cases — [Horizont](https://www.horizont.net/marketing/nachrichten/werbeversprechen-gericht-verbietetcheck24die-nirgendwo-guenstiger-garantie-182526)

### Inferences
- The largest direct writer staying off the portal shows that strong brands with their own direct channel can refuse intermediation. The same choice will face them with third-party AI agents: expose tariffs to agents or not.
- An agent restricted to Check24's inventory inherits Check24's coverage gap. An independent agent that can also query direct-only providers (HUK24 and similar) would have a structural coverage advantage, if those providers allow it.

### Gaps
- Whether HUK-Coburg/HUK24 is listed on Check24 as of November 2026, and which other insurers, energy suppliers or banks stay off: not confirmed by any source found. Unverified recollection: HUK withdrew from comparison portals in 2017.
- How tariff data is integrated (insurer APIs, BiPRO standards, in-house Tarifrechner, energy tariff databases): no source found.
- Exclusive tariffs and portal-only pricing ("Check24-exklusiv"): no source found in this session.

## 6. Legal and regulatory history; competitors

### Takeaway
Courts settled in 2016-17 that Check24 is an insurance broker with full broker information and advice duties online; the "Nirgendwo-Günstiger-Garantie" was fought over repeatedly in Cologne, and the Bundeskartellamt's 2019 sector inquiry documented commission-influenced rankings and thin coverage across portals. The main rival Verivox changed hands in March 2025, sold by ProSiebenSat.1 to Italy's Moltiply Group at an equity value of about EUR 232m, a small fraction of any plausible Check24 valuation.

### Cited Findings
- BVK v. Check24, LG München I, 13 Jul 2016 (37 O 15268/15): advice duties under § 61 VVG also apply to online brokers; a button labelled "Erstinformationen" is insufficient; the initial information must be presented so that the user does not have to search for it; the court nonetheless upheld the basic business model — [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/lgmuencheni-37O1526815-check24-versicherungen-portal-online-makler-beratungspflichten)
- OLG München, 6 Apr 2017 (29 U 3139/16): largely confirmed in the BVK's favour; Check24 accepted the ruling; broker status must be made clear at first business contact, not just in the footer — [Versicherungsbote](https://www.versicherungsbote.de/id/4858384/Check24-akzeptiert-Urteil-des-OLG-Muenchen/); [Cash-Online](https://www.cash-online.de/a/bvk-klage-372888/)
- Both sides claimed victory in BVK v. Check24 — [experten.de](https://www.experten.de/id/4943171/bvk-gegen-check24-beide-seiten-geben-sich-als-sieger/index.pdf)
- After the ruling Check24 points more clearly to its broker status — [Stiftung Warentest](https://www.test.de/Check24-Portal-weist-deutlicher-auf-Maklerstatus-hin-5046048-0/)
- Jubiläumsdeals: LG München I ruled Check24's anniversary discount deals on insurance inadmissible; BVK president Michael Heinz said he expected further violations — [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/lg-muenchen-check-24-jubilaeumsdeals-unzulaessig-rabatte-versicherungen-online-bkv)
- Nirgendwo-Günstiger-Garantie, round 1: LG Köln, 18 Sep 2018 (31 O 376/17), examined whether the guarantee was misleading; Check24 then adapted it (reimbursing the difference) — [Versicherungsbote](https://www.versicherungsbote.de/id/4881134/Check24-Nirgendwo-Guenstiger-HUK-Coburg/)
- Round 2: HUK sued again in April 2019 (LG Köln, 84 O 76/19); the court prohibited the advertising claim, holding that subjective criteria not transparent to consumers were decisive — [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/olg-koeln-84o7619-check24-versicherung-vergleichsportal-huk-coburg-niergendwo-guenstiger-garantie); [Cash-Online](https://www.cash-online.de/a/check24-verliert-gegen-huk-coburg-lg-koeln-verbietet-die-nirgendwo-guenstiger-garantie-535985/)
- Conflicting reports: another report says HUK-Coburg lost against Check24 and that Check24 may keep advertising the guarantee in its then-current form for motor insurance — [Cash-Online](https://www.cash-online.de/a/urteil-huk-coburg-erleidet-niederlage-gegen-check24-470701/). These appear to be different proceedings or instances; the snippets do not allow a reliable timeline.

- Verivox sold in 2025 for roughly what was paid in 2015, a decade of no value creation for the number two. Scale in this market accrues to the leader, consistent with a winner-takes-most dynamic built on brand spend.
- Broker status matters for agents: an AI agent that recommends and concludes insurance for a consumer in Germany would itself fall under broker rules (first-contact disclosure, § 61 VVG advice and documentation duties), exactly the points litigated against Check24.

## 7. Existing automatic switching services (proto-agents)

### Takeaway
Germany already has mandate-based energy switching services with two distinct models: a success fee on savings (Wechselpilot: 25% of expected savings on the first switch, 20% after, capped at EUR 200 per meter, free if nothing is saved) and "free to the consumer", supplier-commission-funded services (Switchup, remind.me). Stiftung Warentest tested nine such services in May-July 2025, named two winners and advised against remind.me, in part because it switches without active consent.

### Cited Findings
- Stiftung Warentest tested nine switching services for electricity and gas from May to July 2025, including Wechselpilot; two services came out ahead — [Stiftung Warentest](https://www.test.de/Strom-Wechselservice-Test-5447465-0/)
- Same test: "We advise against Remind.me"; one criticism is that the switch takes place even without the customer's active consent — [Stiftung Warentest](https://www.test.de/Strom-Wechselservice-Test-5447465-0/)
- Same test, evaluation criterion: if the service receives a commission from the new supplier for brokering the customer, it should be credited to the customer and shown in the offer — [Stiftung Warentest](https://www.test.de/Strom-Wechselservice-Test-5447465-0/)
- Wechselpilot fee (current official page): 25% of the expected saving for the first switch of a newly created meter, 20% from the second switch, capped at EUR 200 per meter; if the customer saves nothing the service is free — [Wechselpilot Servicegebühr](https://wechselpilot.com/ueber-uns/servicegebuehr)
- Older Wechselpilot pages still state 20% of the actual saving after one year, so the fee was raised and the basis moved from actual to expected saving at some point — [Wechselpilot FAQ](https://www.wechselpilot.com/faq/wie-finanziert-sich-wechselpilot/)
- Wechselpilot also sometimes receives commissions from intermediaries and says it often passes part of them to customers as a bonus — [Wechselpilot](https://www.wechselpilot.com/hilfe/wechselservice/wie-finanziert-sich-wechselpilot/)
- Wechselpilot markets annual automatic switching ("automatisch, jährlich") in 2026 and cites a Finanztest long-term test of December 2021 in which its gas switching service was the winner (company's own summary) — [Wechselpilot](https://www.wechselpilot.com/strom/); [Wechselpilot Presse](https://wechselpilot.com/ueber-uns/presse)
- Switchup: free for the consumer; the company receives a commission from the energy suppliers (2016 report; a 2020s comparison article repeats the description) — [deutsche-startups.de, 15 Aug 2016](https://www.deutsche-startups.de/2016/08/15/gas-und-stromanbieter-einfach-wechseln-lassen/); [finanzherr.de](https://finanzherr.de/sparen/stromwechselservice/)
- Cheapenergy24: charges customers a commission on the saving (2016 report) — [deutsche-startups.de, 15 Aug 2016](https://www.deutsche-startups.de/2016/08/15/gas-und-stromanbieter-einfach-wechseln-lassen/)
- remind.me: described as a free switching service for electricity and gas (2021 interview), with long-term partnerships with media companies as a distribution channel — [deutsche-startups.de, 15 Oct 2021](https://www.deutsche-startups.de/2021/10/15/remind-me-wechselservice/); [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/directory/remind-me/)

### Inferences
- The two models map onto the core question for an agent-native Check24: who pays the agent. The success-fee model aligns the agent with the consumer but needs the consumer to pay; the commission model is free but recreates the portal's conflict of interest. Stiftung Warentest's criterion (commission credited to the customer) signals the consumer-protection benchmark.
- Stiftung Warentest's objection to remind.me shows where autonomy limits currently sit in Germany: switching under a standing mandate without per-switch active consent is treated as a defect, not a feature.
- These services stayed confined to energy, a homogeneous product with annual contracts and simple switching rules. No evidence was found of comparable auto-switching at scale in insurance, telecoms or banking.

### Gaps
- Customer numbers, switch volumes, revenue or funding for Wechselpilot, Switchup, remind.me, esave and Cheapenergy24: none found.
- Which two services won the 2025 Stiftung Warentest test, and the detailed ratings: not visible (test.de blocked, article likely paywalled).
- esave business model and current status of Cheapenergy24: not found beyond the 2016 mention.
- Whether Check24's or Verivox's own switching services were included in the 2025 test: not found.
